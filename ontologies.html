Textile 2.0.0

Textile 2.0.0 aligns to the owner-adopted October 2026 CORE baseline, including SOC 2.2.0 in https://w3id.org/eudpp#. Its seven package-specific MIREOT resources (SOC, CON, P_DPP, EVENT, ACTOR, MAT and unchanged SI) are bounded subsets, distinct from the independently published CORE pages. Persistent Textile IRI resolution under w3id.org/eudpp is pending separate CIRPASS-2 and namespace-maintainer coordination; this documentation does not deploy redirects or content negotiation.
