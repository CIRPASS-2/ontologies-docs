Material typec back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/eudpp#MaterialType

Placeholder class for material type classification applicable in a sector or mandated by a delegated act (DA). Potential types could be, on the example of textile sector: cotton, nylon, ...

has material type op
