Carbon Footprintc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/eudpp#CarbonFootprint

An LCIAResult that "means the sum of greenhouse gas emissions and greenhouse gas removals in a product system, expressed as CO equivalents and based on a life cycle assessment using the single impact category of climate change".
