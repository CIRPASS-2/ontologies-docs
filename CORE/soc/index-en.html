IRI: https://w3id.org/eudpp#SubstanceManagementInstruction

An instruction concerning the management of a substance of concern in its SubstancePresence context, including safe use, disassembly, preparation for reuse, reuse, recycling and environmentally sound end-of-life management. Substance of concernc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/eudpp#SubstanceOfConcern

A substance that: (a) meets the criteria laid down in Article 57 of Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 and is identified in accordance with Article 59(1) of that Regulation; (b) is classified in Part 3 of Annex VI to Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 in one of the following hazard classes or hazard categories: (i) carcinogenicity categories 1 and 2; (ii) germ cell mutagenicity categories 1 and 2; (iii) reproductive toxicity categories 1 and 2; (iv) endocrine disruption for human health categories 1 and 2; (v) endocrine disruption for the environment categories 1 and 2; (vi) persistent, mobile and toxic or very persistent, very mobile properties; (vii) persistent, bioaccumulative and toxic or very persistent, very bioaccumulative properties; (viii) respiratory sensitisation category 1; (ix) skin sensitisation category 1; (x) hazardous to the aquatic environment — categories chronic 1 to 4; (xi) hazardous to the ozone layer; (xii) specific target organ toxicity — repeated exposure categories 1 and 2; (xiii) specific target organ toxicity — single exposure categories 1 and 2; (c) is regulated under Regulation (EU) 2019/1021; or (d) negatively affects the reuse and recycling of materials in the product in which it is present.
Is defined by

Substance presencec back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/eudpp#SubstancePresence

A contextual relation entity representing the presence of a substance in relation to a product as placed on the market, including a product, component or spare part. It enables product-dependent location, concentration and instruction information to be represented without treating those data as intrinsic characteristics of the substance itself or classifying the presence as a product property. In the CORE profile the modelling pattern is one SubstancePresence per reported location when different locations require different concentrations; sector-specific extensions may provide explicit reference bases for several calculation perspectives. This class is an ontology modelling abstraction supporting ESPR Article 7(5), rather than a term explicitly defined by the Regulation.
Is defined by
https://w3id.org/eudpp/SOC
Source
http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj
is in domain of
