has LCA verifierop back to ToC or Object Property ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/eudpp#hasLCAVerifier

Links a review to the independent, third-party actor responsible for validating the Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) or Environmental Product Declaration (EPD). Verifier means a person or organisation that carries out the independent verification of an EPD or LCA study, i.e. confirms through objective evidence that the specified requirements are fulfilled, e.g. the PCR and EN 15804+A2. It also covers the critical reviewer of an LCA study and the verifier of a PEF study. Independent verification may be internal or external; EPDs for business-to-consumer communication require third-party verification, also comparative assertions under ISO 14040 and any comparisons under the European Commission’s ILCD Handbook. The type of verification is recorded on the Review (TypeOfReview). ILCD: reviewer (1-3-20-87); [ISO 14025:2006, 3.8, adapted] Review is modelled in the EU DPP CO LCA module. Actor is modelled in the EU DPP CO ACTOR module.
Source
https://www.iso.org/standard/87610.html

Review c
has range
Actor c
