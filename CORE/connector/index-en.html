concerns DPPop back to ToC or Object Property ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/eudpp#concernsDPP

Links a DPP event to the Digital Product Passport whose content, state, or accessibility is affected by the event. This is a cross-module relation between EVENT and P_DPP and is therefore managed in the Connector/mapping layer. DPP is modelled in the EU DPP CO P_DPP module. DPPEvent is modelled in the EU DPP CO EVENT module.

D P P Event c
has range
D P P c
