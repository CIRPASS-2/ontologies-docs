EU DPP Textile Structure Ontology: Description back to ToC

Characteristic classes describe what is observed or declared about a fibre, yarn, fabric or assembly. Concrete numerical values are represented as txtcon:QuantitativeValue instances, while methods and results belong to Textile Assessment and process settings belong to Textile Process.
