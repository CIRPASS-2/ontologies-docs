EU DPP Textile Associated Materials Ontology: Description back to ToC

The module reuses the generic substance identity provided by the Substances of Concern model and the material-of-composition pattern where material occurrences are required.

It does not itself classify a chemical substance as a substance of concern. Regulatory qualification and textile-specific substance occurrence are represented by the Textile SoC module, while fibre composition remains part of Textile Core.

TextileChemicalSubstance specialises CORE Substance without globally classifying generic chemicals as substances of concern. NonTextileComponent specialises the Textile-owned TextileProductPart. Material groups are structural reporting groupings and are not universally products or material occurrences.
