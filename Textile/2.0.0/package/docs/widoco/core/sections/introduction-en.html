Introduction back to ToC

The EU DPP Textile Core Ontology provides the common product-level concepts used across the textile ontology network. Its purpose is to represent the main textile product categories and the information required to describe their composition and classification.

The module includes classes for textile products, textile apparel, fibres, yarns and fabrics, together with controlled vocabularies for textile apparel product types and fibre types. It also represents fibre composition through structured composition components and quantitative values.

The modelling is based on the JRC preparatory study on textiles. Source references and provenance annotations are retained in the ontology to maintain traceability between ontology resources and their documentary basis.
