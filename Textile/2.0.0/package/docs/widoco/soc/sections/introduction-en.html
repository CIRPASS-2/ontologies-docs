Introduction back to ToC

Substances used or found in textile products require contextual information about where they occur, at what concentration and under which regulatory or product-group conditions they are relevant. The Textile SoC module extends the generic EU DPP substance-of-concern model with textile-sector concepts for these purposes.

The module connects substance occurrences to textile material groups, non-textile components, chemical functions, chemical-use categories and textile processes where relevant.
