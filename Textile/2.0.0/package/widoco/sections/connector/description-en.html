EU DPP Textile Connector Ontology: Description back to ToC

The Connector intentionally does not centralise domain-specific operational relations. Instance-level relations remain in the module responsible for the corresponding domain and may be constrained through SHACL.

Weak cross-module relations are represented as annotation properties. They express contextual or typical relationships and do not constitute deterministic causal axioms. Relation polarity and detailed evidence remain associated with the corresponding relation-level documentation rather than being asserted directly on source classes.

The quantitative-value pattern links numerical values to BIPM SI resources without recreating SI units or quantity kinds within the textile namespace.
