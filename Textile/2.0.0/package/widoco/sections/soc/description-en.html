EU DPP Textile Ontology - Textile Substances of Concern Module: Description back to ToC

The module does not classify every textile chemical globally as a substance of concern. Substance-of-concern information is attached to a presence context, allowing the same chemical identity to participate in different product and regulatory contexts.

Textile-specific location concepts distinguish occurrences in fibre-composition material groups, non-textile components, coatings or finishes, printed or dyed regions, adhesive or bonding layers, the whole product or an otherwise unknown location.

The model also supports concentration reporting, textile-specific thresholds, product-group-specific type-(d) qualification and lifecycle-handling information relevant to reuse, recycling and environmentally sound end-of-life treatment.

Positive SoC reports use CORE SubstancePresence and concernsSubstance, with presence-specific qualification applicability. Concentrations use CORE scalar or range carriers. Textile retains separate threshold expression roles, structured locations and denominators, regulatory sources, evaluations and instruction text. Concentration completeness is a separately selected SHACL profile; IDENT is deferred.
