EU DPP Textile Recyclability Ontology: Description back to ToC

Recyclability assessments may refer to barriers, criteria, route-specific conditions, scores and labels. Scores and labels remain method-specific and do not constitute regulatory classifications unless such classifications are subsequently established by an applicable delegated act or standard.

Recycled content is modelled independently from recyclability. A textile product may contain recycled material without being recyclable through a given route, while a recyclable product may contain no recycled material.

Actual recycling executions and recovered outputs are represented separately from the prospective assessment of recyclability.

The recycled-content model remains Textile-owned. The obsolete RecycledMaterialsUse annotation has been removed. The substance-presence range IRI is retained and now reaches the migrated CORE presence pattern. Comparison outcomes do not establish regulatory compliance.
