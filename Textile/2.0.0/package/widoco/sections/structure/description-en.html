EU DPP Textile Structure Ontology: Description back to ToC

Characteristic classes describe what is observed or declared about a textile fibre, yarn, fabric or assembly. Concrete numerical values are represented through txtcon:QuantitativeValue, while qualitative values may be recorded separately where an appropriate controlled vocabulary is not available.

The module deliberately separates product characteristics from process parameters. A real-world quantity may therefore occur as a process target in Textile Process and as a product characteristic in Textile Structure, but these semantic roles remain explicit.

Assessment methods and results belong to Textile Assessment, while observed degradation phenomena belong to Textile Durability. This avoids conflating intrinsic or declared structure characteristics with observations obtained during durability testing.
