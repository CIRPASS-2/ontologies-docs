EU DPP Textile Repairability Ontology: Description back to ToC

Repairability assessments collect evidence about relevant product aspects and contextual conditions. Their results record evidence-based outcomes and service configurations without asserting that the assessed textile product is objectively repairable in every context.

Repair-related operations such as repair, upgrade, refurbishment and remanufacturing are connected to concrete textile process executions. Textile-specific life-extension events also bridge these process executions with the semantic product-event classes provided by the EU DPP EVENT model.

EPCIS or other technical event carriers remain outside this textile module and are governed by the EVENT layer.
